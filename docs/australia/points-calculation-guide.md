# Points Calculation Guide

This document details how points are calculated for the General Skilled Migration (GSM) visas. 

> [!NOTE]
> **Source Information:** The points outlined below are based on the official [Department of Home Affairs Points Table](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-nominated-190/points-table).

## Age
| Age Group | Points Awarded |
| :--- | :--- |
| 18 to 24 years | 25 points |
| 25 to 32 years | 30 points |
| 33 to 39 years | 25 points |
| 40 to 44 years | 15 points |
| 45 years and over | 0 points |

## English Language Skills
| Proficiency Level | Points Awarded |
| :--- | :--- |
| Competent English | 0 points |
| Proficient English | 10 points |
| Superior English | 20 points |

## Skilled Employment Experience (past 10 years)
> [!NOTE]
> **Maximum Points Cap:** The maximum combined points you can claim for any combination of Australian and overseas skilled employment is 20 points.

### Overseas Employment (Outside Australia)
| Experience | Points Awarded |
| :--- | :--- |
| Less than 3 years | 0 points |
| 3 to 4 years | 5 points |
| 5 to 7 years | 10 points |
| 8 years or more | 15 points |

### Australian Employment (Inside Australia)
| Experience | Points Awarded |
| :--- | :--- |
| Less than 1 year | 0 points |
| 1 to 2 years | 5 points |
| 3 to 4 years | 10 points |
| 5 to 7 years | 15 points |
| 8 years or more | 20 points |

## Educational Qualifications Points Table

You can only claim points for your highest qualification. An overseas degree (e.g., from Nepal) is fully eligible for these points as long as it is recognized as equivalent to Australian standards by your designated skills assessing authority.

| Highest Completed Qualification | Points Awarded |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ph.D. / Doctorate | 20 points |
| Bachelor Degree (or a Master's Degree) | 15 points |
| Trade Qualification / Advanced Diploma / Diploma | 10 points |
| Any lesser qualification (recognized by assessment) | 5 points |

## Additional Categories & Legal Requirements

| Category | Points Awarded | Legal Requirement to Get Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Specialist Educational Qualification** | 10 points | Must be a Research Master's or PhD completed inside Australia in a STEM field. |
| **Australian Study Requirement** | 5 points | Requires physically completing a registered course of at least 16 calendar months (2 academic years) inside Australia. |
| **Professional Year in Australia** | 5 points | Must complete a formal 12-month professional internship program inside Australia after graduating from an Australian university. |
| **Credentialled Community Language** | 5 points | Requires passing an official NAATI CCL interpreting/translating exam inside Australia or online from overseas. (Note: You do not get the points until you pass the exam). |
| **Study in Regional Australia** | 5 points | Must live and study at a campus located in a designated regional area inside Australia. |
