# Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190)

> [!WARNING]
> **General Skilled Migration (GSM) Category**
> Submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI) and receiving an official invitation is a mandatory legal requirement for this visa. You are strictly forbidden from applying for the actual visa unless the government invites you first.

This is a permanent visa for skilled workers who are nominated by an Australian state or territory government.

## Key Features
- Stay in Australia permanently.
- Work and study in Australia.
- Enroll in Australia's public health care scheme, Medicare.
- Sponsor eligible relatives for permanent residence.
- Travel to and from Australia for 5 years.
- If eligible, become an Australian citizen.

## Points Test
You must score at least 65 points to be invited to apply. Points are awarded for:
- Age
- English language skills
- Skilled employment experience (Australian and Overseas)
- Educational qualifications
- Specialist education qualifications
- Australian study requirement
- Professional Year in Australia
- Credentialled community language
- Study in regional Australia
- Partner skills
- State nomination (Subclass 190 gives 5 points automatically)
