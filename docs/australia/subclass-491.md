# Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491)

> [!WARNING]
> **General Skilled Migration (GSM) Category**
> Submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI) and receiving an official invitation is a mandatory legal requirement for this visa. You are strictly forbidden from applying for the actual visa unless the government invites you first.

This is a provisional visa for skilled workers who want to live and work in regional Australia.

## Key Features
- Stay in Australia for 5 years.
- Live, work and study in a designated regional area of Australia.
- Travel to and from Australia as many times as you want, while the visa is valid.
- Apply for permanent residence after 3 years from the time your visa is granted.

## Points Test
You must score at least 65 points to be invited.
State/Territory nomination or sponsorship by an eligible relative living in regional Australia awards 15 points towards your total score.
